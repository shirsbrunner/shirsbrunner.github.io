## Life, Work and Projects
It's getting cold on the bike and leaves are changing colour. There is more rain and more wind. Overall, I like autumn, not only because of the food but also because of the feel of the weather on my face. 

Glyphs 4 is really nice. Still a bit buggy here and there, but I love working on typefaces. Apart from this I found time and energy to build a list of PWA. There is a new one after all, a Web Radio Player. It's my most used personal app at the moment. Before leaving the house, we listen to ABC SRF 3 - a game that's on the radio in the morning. But just listening to random music, news and other radioshows feels refreshing. 

Work is fun. I have a lot of different projects with different challenges. From process via politics to design tasks. Doing a lot of testing and studies again and working with statistics software again. It does not feel bad to have switched to an inhouse designer role.  

I've been investing in some type books - Sofie Beier's Type tricks. The type design one is mint. And did you know about [Flexagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexagon)?
